01 — Problem

Good candidates get filtered out before a human ever reads them

Struggling to get a resume past the Applicant Tracking System (ATS) or land an interview is a common failure point — often the resume itself, not the candidate, is the bottleneck.

02 — Approach

NLP + GPT-3.5-turbo in a Streamlit interface

The app is a one-stop, AI-powered solution for crafting a resume that gets noticed — pairing NLP-based analysis with GPT-3.5-turbo inside a Streamlit front end so the feedback loop stays fast and interactive.
